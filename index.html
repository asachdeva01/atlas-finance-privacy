Atlas Finance — Privacy Policy

Registered with Plaid as “Atlas Core Finance”. Last updated September 16, 2026.

Atlas Finance is a private, personal finance app run by its owner for the owner and invited household members. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

What we collect

  • Account balances, holdings, transactions, and liabilities from institutions you connect through Plaid or Robinhood. Access is read-only. The app never moves money and never places trades.
  • Your name, email, and phone, used only to sign you in and address you.
  • Nothing else. No advertising identifiers, no analytics trackers, no location.

How it is used

  • To show you your own money: net worth, spending, holdings, and the rules you set.
  • To write reports and summaries for you. Numbers are sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) to write prose. Bank credentials and access tokens are never sent anywhere except the institution's own API.

Who can see it

  • Only you. Each account is scoped to its owner. There is no admin view of anyone else's data, and nothing is sold, shared, or licensed to third parties.
  • The server runs on hardware the owner controls, reachable only over a private network (Tailscale) with device authentication.

How it is protected

  • All traffic uses TLS 1.2 or better. Access tokens are encrypted at rest with a key held only on the server; the server disk is fully encrypted.
  • Sign-in uses a password plus Face ID / Touch ID passkeys (WebAuthn). Connecting a bank requires a fresh passkey check.

Deletion and retention

  • Disconnecting an institution revokes its access at Plaid or Robinhood immediately and deletes the stored token and its data from this app.
  • Deleting your account removes all of your data.
  • Data is kept only while an institution is connected and your account exists. Backups, if any, are encrypted and expire within 30 days.

Your rights and contact

  • You can see, export, or delete everything the app holds about you at any time by contacting the owner: a.sachdeva2021@gmail.com.
